About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu brings together the best local stall for three days of eating, music and culture by the river. Visitor can try famous dishes such as nasi dagang, keropok lekor and laksam, prepare by home cooks and small business from all over the state

The festival also support local enterpreneurs and student. Cooking demonstrations are held every evening, and all stall follow the festival's green guideline by using reusable or biodegradable packaging.

Free entry for all UMT students - show your matric card at the entrance
