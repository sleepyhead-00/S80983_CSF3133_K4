About Us

Welcome to Mania Food Fest the ultimate playground for food lovers! We are a high-energy culinary celebration dedicated to bringing together the boldest flavors, the trendiest street eats, and the most passionate foodies under one roof. Born out of a pure obsession for great food and great vibes, Mania Food Fest is more than just a place to eat; it’s an experience. From mouth-watering local favorites to innovative fusion dishes, we curate the ultimate feast paired with live music, entertainment, and unforgettable memories. Come hungry, leave happy, and join the food mania!

Activity 1 - Cooking Competition

Students must form a team of two and cook a creative dish within 45 minutes based on the secret theme revealed on event day. On the day of the competition, you need to bring your own basic cooking utensils, such as knives, cutting boards, and frying pans, as well as your own specialty plates for food presentation. The organizers will provide the main ingredients, pantry staples, and a portable gas stove at your designated cooking station. You must arrive 30 minutes before the clock starts for a safety briefing, keep your workstation clean throughout the battle, and present your final plate to the judges before the timer hits zero.

Activity 2 - Food Tasting Experience

Students must prepare a signature dish in advance and bring enough portions for the judges and visitors to sample. On event day, your main role is to set up an attractive display table and handle the final plating or assembly of your dish. You need to prepare and bring all your own serving materials, including mini sample cups, toothpicks, spoons, and napkins. The organizers will provide a standard booth table, but you must bring any special equipment needed to keep your food hot or cold. Lastly, be ready to welcome the crowd and confidently explain the flavors and inspiration behind your food during the tasting session
